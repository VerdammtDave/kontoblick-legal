Privacy Policy

Deutsch · Kontoblick · Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. What Kontoblick is

Kontoblick is a personal finance application that runs locally on the user's own computer. It shows account balances and transactions from the user's own bank accounts. It is operated by a private individual for personal, non-commercial use and has no server of its own.

2. Controller

David Böckler, Germany
Contact: kontoblick@laudymedia.de

3. Data we process

  • Account information retrieved through the Enable Banking API after the user has explicitly authorised access at their bank: account name, IBAN, balances, and transactions (date, amount, counterparty name, payment reference).
  • Enable Banking credentials (application ID and private key) entered by the user.
  • User settings such as selected months and categories.

4. Where the data is stored

All data is stored only on the user's own device, in an encrypted file (AES-256-GCM). The encryption key is kept in the operating system's secure credential store. No data is sent to the operator of Kontoblick, and there is no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.

5. Who receives data

Kontoblick communicates directly from the user's device with Enable Banking (api.enablebanking.com) to retrieve account information, and with the user's bank during authorisation. Enable Banking acts as the account information service provider; its own privacy policy applies to that processing. Data is not sold, shared or disclosed to any other party.

6. Legal basis

Processing takes place on the user's own instruction and consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR). Access to bank data is always granted by the user at their bank and can be withdrawn at any time.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until the user deletes it. Disconnecting a bank in the application removes the connection; uninstalling the application and deleting its data folder removes all stored data. Bank-side consent can additionally be revoked at the user's bank.

8. Your rights

Because the operator does not receive or hold the user's data, access, correction and deletion are performed directly in the application. For any question you can contact the controller above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application changes. The date above shows the latest version.
